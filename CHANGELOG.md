# Changelog

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-19

### Changed (Community Edition Scope)
- Scoped open-source GitHub Community Edition to **6 core visual categories** (`Stickman 3D`, `House Cutaway`, `Natural Earth Maps`, `Science Paper Cutout & Comic`, `Cartoon Stage`, and `2D Motion Graphics`) with up to **60s** duration.
- Slimmed `assets/motion-engine.js` to Engine Lite for faster load times.
- Moved the 10 advanced studio categories (3D Monuments, Cutaway Jet & Piston Engines, Astronomy GLSL/Solar System, Ocean 3D, Wildlife, Anatomy, Islamic Heritage, History, Geography, Folklore), 10-minute long-form architecture, Auto-Synced Karaoke Captions, `.srt` export, and Dual-Aspect Batch Builder to **MotionCraft Pro Pack**.
